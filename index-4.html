One platform · Two fulfilment paths

We coordinate. We deliver.

Get It. works alongside existing logistics networks and adds its own last-mile capacity where a partner-approved handover makes sense.

PARTNER-COORDINATED

Use the courier's network

When the courier can fulfil the selected slot, Get It. coordinates the booking, customer updates and delivery visibility. The courier retains parcel custody.

GET IT. FULFILLED

Use our contract riders

When the partner agrees to transfer the parcel, Get It. assigns a contract rider, manages the final-mile delivery and captures proof of delivery.

Designed to scale responsibly

Start focused. Expand by zone.

Begin with a controlled Whitefield pilot, validate delivery density and unit economics, then expand into nearby Bengaluru zones as capacity and partner coverage grow.

Phase 1 · Validate

2–3 seller or logistics partners, limited service area, small contract rider pool.

Phase 2 · Optimise

Improve slot capacity, route batching, customer support and partner integrations.

Phase 3 · Expand

Add nearby zones only after each operating area shows reliable service and positive contribution.
